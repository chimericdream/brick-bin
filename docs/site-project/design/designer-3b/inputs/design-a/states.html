The brick, at three scales only

Text size

A 1×1 brick, 0.8em wide. Bullets, the section chip in navigation, the current-section marker in “On this page”. Never decoration on its own.

Component

Studs 12–28px wide on things you can press or that stand for a thing you own: buttons, placeholders, step numbers, page heads, the parts box, cards.

Architecture

Studs 28–48px on whole bands: the hero stack, the one plate per page, the footer wall, the 404 wall. At most one of these per screen.

Sorted by colour: each colour is a section

Like a parts bin sorted by colour, the bright bricks tell you where you are. They don't change at night; the room around them does. They are never used for decoration elsewhere.
