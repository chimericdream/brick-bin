Short answers to fair questions

The short answer comes first. The reasons follow, for anyone who wants them.

  1. Is this a LEGO app?

    No. Brick Bin works with any brand of building brick, including Mould King, CaDA, COBI, BlueBrixx and Lumibricks. It isn't affiliated with the LEGO Group.

    The optional catalog lookup covers LEGO sets because that's what Rebrickable's data describes. Sets from other brands are added by hand.

  2. Does it need the internet?

    Not day to day. Your data stays on your server, nothing loads from a CDN, and the app makes no background downloads.

    It goes online only when someone asks it to: an admin importing the catalog, a cover fetched for a set you add from the catalog, or a PDF you ask it to save from a web address.

  3. Where does the catalog come from?

    Rebrickable. You import their data yourself, onto your own server.

    Nothing is fetched on a schedule. An admin presses the button, at most once every 48 hours. The import tutorial walks through it.

  4. Can my kids see prices?

    No. Kid profiles are read-only, with no prices and no shopping list. They can still manage their own wishlist.

    Sets you mark as hidden look, to a kid profile, as if they don't exist. That rule lives on the server and is covered by tests, so it doesn't depend on a button being tucked out of sight. Birthday presents are safe.

  5. Can I run it without Tailscale?

    Yes. Run the production compose file and open port 3000.

    We recommend Tailscale because it gives you HTTPS and a tidy address with very little work. Running without it is covered in the hosting docs.

  6. How do I back it up?

    Three things: database dumps, cover images and instruction PDFs.

    The dumps skip the catalog tables and the downloaded catalog images aren't backed up, because both can be imported again. After a restore, run a catalog import. Backups in detail.

  7. Can I track loose bricks?

    Not yet. Version 1 tracks sets, the copies you own of them, and the pieces each copy is missing.

    The rest of the “not yet” list is under Not in version 1.

  8. What does it cost?

    Nothing. Brick Bin is free and open source under the MIT licence. There's no account with us and no subscription.

    You do supply the server, the disk and the bricks.

  9. Who can see that I bought something off a wishlist?

    Only you. The “I bought this” mark is private to the person who made it. Admins can't see it and it never appears in the activity log.

  10. Does it update itself?

    The app does. The database doesn't. Watchtower checks for a new app image every five minutes. Postgres is never updated automatically.

Not in version 1

  • Loose-brick inventory
  • Owned minifigures
  • A build log
  • Loans
  • Ratings
  • Guest share links
  • Price-guide integrations
  • Currencies other than US dollars

If one of these is the thing you need, Brick Bin isn't ready for you yet. Better to hear it now than after the catalog download.
