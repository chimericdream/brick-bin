Short answers to fair questions

Filed like cards in a drawer. Open one at a time; the first is already out.

BrandsIs this a LEGO app?

No. Brick Bin works with sets from any brand: LEGO, Mould King, CaDA, COBI, BlueBrixx, Lumibricks, and anything else you can type in by hand. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the LEGO Group.

The optional catalog lookup uses Rebrickable's LEGO data, which is why LEGO sets are the quickest to add.

InternetDoes it need the internet?

Not to run. Brick Bin lives on your server and your devices reach it over your own network. The app goes online only when someone asks it to: importing the catalog, fetching a set's cover, or saving a PDF from a web address. It makes no background downloads.

Tailscale and Watchtower, if you use them, talk to their own servers.

CatalogWhere does the catalog come from?

From Rebrickable's published data. An admin imports it from Brick Bin's catalog page, at most once every 48 hours, and nothing is fetched on a schedule. See Import the LEGO catalog.

KidsCan my kids see prices?

Not on a kid profile. Kid profiles are read-only, show no prices and no shopping list, and sets you mark hidden look as if they don't exist. The server enforces this, so it holds even if someone types an address by hand, and tests check it. Kids can still manage their own wishlist.

TailscaleCan I run it without Tailscale?

Yes. For a quick try-out, run the production compose file and open port 3000. Tailscale is the recommended route for everyday use because the sidecar gives you HTTPS at a tidy address. Hosting without Tailscale has the details.

BackupsHow do I back it up?

Back up database dumps, cover images and instruction PDFs. The dumps skip the catalog tables, and the downloaded catalog images aren't backed up, because both can be imported again. After a restore, run a catalog import. Backups walks through it.

Loose bricksCan I track loose bricks?

Not yet. Version 1 tracks sets, the copies you own and their missing pieces. Loose-brick inventory isn't in it, and neither are owned minifigures, a build log, loans, ratings, guest share links, price-guide integrations or currencies other than US dollars.

CostWhat does it cost?

Nothing. Brick Bin is free and open source. You provide the computer it runs on and the disk it fills.

WishlistsWho can see that I bought something off a wishlist?

Only you. An “I bought this” mark is visible to the person who made it and nobody else: not the wish's owner, not admins, not the activity log. When the box arrives, “We got it” turns the wish into a set.

UpdatesDoes it update itself?

The app does, if you use the recommended stack: Watchtower pulls a new Brick Bin image every five minutes when there is one. Postgres is never updated automatically.

Didn't find your question? The docs go into more detail, and you can ask on the project's issue tracker.

Not in version 1

  • Loose-brick inventory
  • Owned minifigures
  • A build log
  • Loans
  • Ratings
  • Guest share links
  • Price-guide integrations
  • Currencies other than US dollars

If one of these is the thing you need, Brick Bin isn't ready for you yet.
