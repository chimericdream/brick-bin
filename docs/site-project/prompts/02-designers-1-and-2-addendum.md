# Addendum for Designers 1 and 2

Sent together with `01-design-brief-part-1.md`.

You are one of two designers working independently on the same brief. You will not see the other's work, and you must not look for it. Choose a bold, specific direction and commit to it. Do not hedge toward a safe average, because the human wants two genuinely different options to compare. Briefly state your concept, and why it fits, in your handoff notes.
